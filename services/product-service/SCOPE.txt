Product Service + ekt.kz integration (Module 1)

Planned work based on ARCHITECTURE.md:
- Build the Python 3.12 / FastAPI service with PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy,
  Pydantic and httpx.
- Connect to the ekt.kz Product API using server-only Basic Auth.
- Synchronize the paginated catalog and normalize product characteristics,
  preserving raw values and explicitly flagging conflicting data.
- Provide product search, live product details, prices and warehouse stock.
- Find and rank analog candidates with compatibility checks and differences;
  do not claim compatibility when critical characteristics are uncertain.
- Expose the architecture's internal v1 search, detail, availability and
  analog endpoints, with consistent errors and upstream timeout handling.
- Later support cart integration through live availability checks.
  The cart adapter and cart mutations belong to Module 3 in the architecture.

Scope excludes UI, AI/LLM logic, chat history and document processing.
Current status: implemented in the Product Service directory.
